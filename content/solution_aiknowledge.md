## AI Knowledge Assistant

> Turn your company's knowledge into an intelligent assistant.

Make documents, SOPs, policies, manuals, and internal knowledge instantly accessible through natural-language conversations.

### Overview

**Stop Searching. Start Asking.**
Important business knowledge is often scattered across PDFs, documents, spreadsheets, wikis, and internal systems.

An AI Knowledge Assistant gives your team a single interface to find and understand that information.

Instead of searching through dozens of documents, employees can simply ask a question and get an answer based on your company's knowledge.
