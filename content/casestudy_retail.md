** AI Analytics · Natural Language · Business Intelligence **

### The Challenge

** Business data is valuable — but accessing it isn't always easy.**

Retail teams often rely on dashboards, spreadsheets, and manually prepared reports to answer everyday questions.

Simple questions can require analysts or technical teams to prepare queries and reports before the answer becomes available.

### The Approach

**Make business data accessible through natural language.**
Instead of requiring users to understand SQL or navigate complex dashboards, the system allows users to ask questions using everyday language.

For example:
> "Which products had the highest sales last month?"
or:
> "Which stores experienced a significant decline in sales?"

The AI translates the business question into a data query, retrieves the relevant information, and presents the result in a human-readable form.
