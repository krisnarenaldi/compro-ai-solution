## CV Screening
** Screen Candidates Faster with AI **
> An AI-powered recruitment workflow that analyzes resumes against job requirements and helps recruiters identify relevant candidates faster.

** AI Screening · HR Automation · Workflow Automation **

### The Challenge

** Recruiters often spend significant time reviewing resumes before reaching the shortlist.**

For roles receiving many applications, manually comparing each candidate against job requirements can become repetitive and time-consuming.

The challenge is not simply reading resumes — it's consistently evaluating how well each candidate matches the role.

### The Approach

**Automate the first layer of candidate screening.**
The system uses AI to process candidate resumes, compare candidate information against job requirements, and produce a structured assessment for recruiters.

AI handles the repetitive first-pass analysis while recruiters remain responsible for the final decision.

### How It Works

       ↓

       ↓

### Key Capabilities
**Resume Parsing**  
Extract relevant information from candidate resumes.

**Requirement Matching**  
Compare candidate experience and skills against the job requirements.

**AI Assessment**  
Generate a structured evaluation of candidate relevance.

**Candidate Ranking**  
Help recruiters prioritize candidates for further review.

**Automated Workflow**  
Move candidate information through the screening process automatically.

**Recruiter-in-the-Loop**  
Keep final candidate selection under human control.


### Technology

**LLM / Generative AI**  
Analyze and interpret resume content.

**Workflow Automation**  
Automate the movement of candidate data through the screening process.

**Google Sheets / Structured Data**  
Store and organize screening results.

**AI Integration**  
Connect recruitment inputs with AI analysis.


### Outcome
** Less time spent on repetitive resume review. **

The workflow helps recruitment teams:

-   Process large numbers of resumes more efficiently
-   Apply a consistent initial screening process
-   Quickly identify candidates worth reviewing
-   Reduce repetitive manual data processing
-   Keep recruiters focused on interviews and final evaluation
